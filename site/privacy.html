From the First Cone

Privacy

Effective 25 November 2026. Applies to From the First Cone for iPhone.

From the First Cone collects nothing. I run no server for it, so there is nowhere for your data to go.

What the app does not do

  • It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting.
  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It contains no code from any other developer: no analytics, advertising or other third-party libraries.
  • It makes no network requests. The drills, the practice plans and the bell are all inside the app.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your changes to a practice

If you swap, add or remove a drill, the app remembers your list for that sport and focus, so it is there next time. If you pick a different focus for a day, it remembers that too. Both stay on your phone, in the standard iOS settings storage, and go into your iCloud or computer backups along with the rest of your phone’s settings. Restore default practice clears a changed drill list; picking the day’s planned focus again clears a focus change.

Timers and tallies

These are not saved. They are gone when the practice ends.

Alarms

On iOS 26 and later, the app asks whether it may set alarms the first time you start a practice. If you allow it, each drill’s timer sets an alarm with iOS, so the drill’s end rings even with the phone locked. The alarm is set on your phone and goes nowhere else. You can turn the permission off in the Settings app.

One thing that leaves the app

Many drills link to the organization that published them. If you tap that link, the app hands the address to your web browser, and the browser loads the page under its own privacy rules. The app sends nothing.

What Apple shares with me

If you chose in Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send me crash reports and usage figures for the app. They do not tell me who you are, and the app adds nothing to them.

Sharing and keeping

The app does not send, share, sell or keep any personal data, because it never collects any. If you delete the app, your saved practices are deleted with it.

The app does no tracking, so it has nothing to change when your browser or phone sends a Do Not Track signal.

Children

The app is for the adults who coach. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version replaces this page and the date at the top changes with it.
